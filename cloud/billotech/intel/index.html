Numbers that carry their own error

Not a prettier map

A naive tool says "214 hours at home." This says 214 hours of counted presence, and flags that some raw dwell spans a data gap too long to trust — so you know what not to lean on.

Device vs person

The dot is a device's best guess. Containment is the probability the real position sat inside the place. The halo ratio splits the time you were there from the activity orbiting nearby.

The standard

Read the code. Trust the number.

Digital-forensics guidance on location evidence is blunt about it: accuracy radii, confidence scores, and the gap between device presence and person presence must always be disclosed, never assumed away. That sentence is, line for line, what this engine implements.
