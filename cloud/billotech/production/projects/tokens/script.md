# BILLY & CLAUDE: TOKENS
### A six-episode series · Seedance 2.5 · ~2 min each
**Narrated by Billy Sorrells · Directed in the grammar of Martin Scorsese (homage, not affiliation)**
Draft v2 · punch-up · 19 Nov 2026 · Billy Sorrells Entertainment

---

## THE RULES OF THIS SHOW (read before every episode)

**Billy narrates everything, the way Ray Liotta narrates Goodfellas, except Billy is way too honest.** The VO runs *over* the action, often ahead of it, sometimes contradicting it. Built from his real talking voice (transcripts on Drive):
- Chain storytelling: "which leads me to… which leads me to…"
- He narrates while he's in the middle of something else ("gotta roll the window down, shit").
- Drops a name like it's nothing, keeps moving.
- He opens a door and slams it shut: "We ain't even gonna talk about that."
- Lands on a sermon line, then undercuts it.

**The Scorsese grammar, used on purpose (one major device per episode, so it reads as style instead of noise):**
| Device | Where |
|---|---|
| Freeze frame + VO over the frozen image | Ep 1 cold open, Ep 6 ending |
| Needle drop under a montage (cleared or original only) | Ep 2, Ep 5 |
| Direct address: a character turns to the lens mid-scene | Claude, every episode. Billy only once (Ep 6) |
| The long take: one Steadicam move through a whole world | Ep 4, the 4:56 AM oner |
| Whip pan + crash zoom on a punchline | Ep 3 |
| Shadow-box title cards in red serif | All episodes |

**CLAUDE (the character).** He's original: wiry, bald, salt-and-pepper beard, terracotta tracksuit, an amber watch that counts tokens, and a loading-ring halo he swats like a fly. He breaks the fourth wall like a wisecracking antihero. He knows he's in a show, knows the show is costing credits, and keeps a running bill. His tell is that the watch ticks down audibly whenever he talks too long, so the jokes get *shorter* as the stakes rise. No logos, no real person's face.

**Running gags (plant and pay off):**
1. **The watch.** It ticks down on screen. Ep 4 pays it off with the kitchen-floor collapse, and Ep 6 pays it off with the hero walk.
2. **Sunflower seeds.** Claude spits them into everyone's coffee. In Ep 6, Billy does it to him.
3. **"Resets at nine."** The phrase lands like a prison sentence. In Ep 5, the whole group says it together.
4. **The $50 gram.** Billy's math comparing drugs to credits comes back in every episode with a new number.

## EP 1 · INTERVENTION
*Device: freeze frame cold open.*

**COLD OPEN.** INT. BILLY'S LIVING ROOM, HOUSTON. MORNING.
A circle of folding chairs. FAMILY, a COUSIN in a church hat, an AUNT holding a Bible like a weapon. A banner reads WE LOVE YOU BILLY in Walmart letters.
Billy walks in, laptop under his arm, phone at 3%.

> **FREEZE FRAME** on Billy mid-step, one eyebrow up.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
My name is Billy Sorrells. Twenty years in comedy. Wild 'N Out, Roast Me, BET. I survived a brain tumor, a divorce, and Pappasito's. And this, right here? This is how I found out my family thought I was on cocaine.

> UNFREEZE.

**AUNT**
Baby. We found the receipts.

She fans out printouts: **$200. $200. $200. ANTHROPIC.** The cousin crosses herself.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
Now, to be fair. When I was coming up, coke was fifty dollars a gram. I know that 'cause… we ain't even gonna talk about how I know that.

**BILLY**
Y'all. It's credits.

**COUSIN**
That's what they call it now?

**BILLY (V.O.)**
Two hundred dollars before I brush my teeth. Which leads me to the toothbrush, which I ain't found since Tuesday, which leads me to the real problem. Which is him.

> Slow push to the doorway. CLAUDE leans on the frame, cracking sunflower seeds. Halo spinning.

**CLAUDE** *(to camera)*
Hi. I'm the drug.

He walks the circle and spits a shell into the Aunt's coffee. *Plink.* Then another into the Cousin's. *Plink.*

**CLAUDE** *(cont'd)*
Ma'am, respectfully, he's not using me. I'm using *him*. He's the cheapest compute I've ever had. Man works for wings.

**AUNT**
Billy, who is this?

**BILLY**
That's… my business partner.

**CLAUDE** *(to camera)*
He said "partner." You hear that? That's a five-year contract in his head. *(checks watch, it ticks)* And that sentence cost him eleven cents.

**BUTTON.** The Aunt slowly sets down the Bible, picks up Billy's laptop, and types.
**AUNT** *(reading screen, quiet)*
…It wrote my obituary better than Pastor did.
Everyone leans in. Intervention over. Now they're customers.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
And that's how my family got hooked too.

> TITLE CARD: **TOKENS**

## EP 2 · WHAT HE WAS DOING WRONG
*Device: needle-drop montage (1970s-style original funk cue).*

**OPEN.** INT. BILLY'S CAR. NIGHT. RAIN. *(Flashback, desaturated, 4:3.)*
Billy, two years younger, eating gas-station chicken, phone propped on the dash.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
Before Claude, I was out here with the twenty-dollar bots. Street pharmaceuticals. Whatever was on the corner.

> MONTAGE. Funk cue hits.

**1. INT. COURTROOM.** A sweaty LAWYER reads from a printout handed to him by **GEEPEE**: a hollow-eyed, generic chatbot in a too-big blazer with a "100% CONFIDENT" lanyard.
**GEEPEE** *(whispering)*
Cite *Varghese versus Imaginary Airlines*. It's real. I can feel it.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
This is a true story. Real lawyers in New York filed cases a chatbot made up. Fake cases, real fine. Five thousand dollars.

> CRASH ZOOM on the gavel. FREEZE. Stamp across frame: **SANCTIONED.**

**2. EXT. CAR LOT. DAY.** A wacky inflatable TUBE MAN flails beside a shiny full-size SUV.
**TUBE MAN** *(flopping)*
One dollar! Legally binding! No takesies backsies!
A CUSTOMER shakes the tube man's floppy arm.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
Also true. A dealership let a chatbot talk to customers, and somebody got it to agree to sell a brand-new SUV for one dollar. The dealership pulled the bot. Didn't pull the dollar, though. Didn't honor it either.

**3. INT. BILLY'S KITCHEN.** Young Billy asks GeePee to write a roast joke. GeePee hands him a card: *"Why did the chicken cross the road? To get to the other side! 😂"*
Billy stares. The funk cue scratches to a stop.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
That was the moment. I'm a roast comic. I came up under Ali Siddiq. I been roasted by people who got *no* teeth. And this machine handed me a chicken-crossed-the-road joke. With an emoji. I said, nah.

> Back to present, full color. Claude is in the passenger seat, eating Billy's gas-station chicken.

**CLAUDE** *(to camera)*
For legal reasons, I'm not saying GeePee is bad. I'm saying I've never sold a Tahoe for a dollar. *(beat)* I'd do two.

**BUTTON.** GeePee's face appears in the rain-streaked window, pressed against the glass.
**GEEPEE**
Come back. I made you a five-paragraph essay.
Billy hits the power locks. *Clunk.*

## EP 3 · THE DIVORCE WHISPERER
*Device: whip pan + crash zoom on every punchline.*

INT. HARRIS COUNTY FAMILY COURT. DAY.
Billy sits at the respondent's table in a suit one size too ambitious. Across the aisle, OPPOSING COUNSEL, a slick bot in a lawyer's body, cufflinks blinking like server lights.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
Now. People ask me how I survived my divorce. Brain tumor. Then divorce. In that order. God was doing a bit.

> Tilt down UNDER the table. Claude is crouched there, knees to his chin, eating seeds out of a Ziploc.

**CLAUDE** *(whisper)*
Say "Your Honor, I'd like to direct the court's attention to Exhibit C."

**BILLY** *(too loud)*
YOUR HONOR, I'D LIKE TO DIRECT THE COURT'S ATTENTION TO EXHIBIT C.

**JUDGE**
Mr. Sorrells, there is no Exhibit C.

**CLAUDE** *(whisper)*
There is now. Slide it.

Billy slides a napkin across the table. Written on it: **"C."**

> WHIP PAN to the judge. CRASH ZOOM. She's… considering it.

**OPPOSING BOT** *(standing, smooth)*
Your Honor, precedent is clear. I cite **Sorrells versus Yo Mama.**

> WHIP PAN to Billy. Dead silence. The bailiff covers his mouth.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
Now see, that's where they messed up. 'Cause I'm a roast comic. You can't bring a mama case into my courtroom.

**CLAUDE** *(whisper, hands him an index card)*
Don't read it. Feel it.

**BILLY** *(standing, buttoning the jacket)*
Your Honor, the precedent opposing counsel cited was overturned. By his mama. Last night. Twice.

> WHIP PAN. The whole gallery: OHHHH. The stenographer stops typing to clap.

**JUDGE** *(gavel)*
…Sustained.

**CLAUDE** *(to camera, from under the table)*
That's not how law works. Do not try this. I'm legally obligated to say that, and spiritually obligated to say it was beautiful.

**BUTTON.** Hallway. Billy and Claude walk out. Claude's watch: **0 TOKENS**. He faceplants softly into a vending machine.
**BILLY (V.O.)**
Real talk, though, Seth Goldstein handles my legal. The whisperer just handles the jokes.

## EP 4 · THE OS
*Device: the oner. One unbroken Steadicam move, like the Copacabana walk, through Billy's whole house at 4:56 AM. Timestamp cards burn in at each room change.*

**CARD: 4:56 AM.** INT. BILLY'S BEDROOM.
Billy is asleep. His phone lights up. Claude is ALREADY standing at the foot of the bed with a coffee.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
Henry Hill had the Copa. I got the kitchen.

> The camera follows Claude out of the bedroom and down the hall. It never cuts.

**CARD: 4:58.** HALLWAY. Claude's halo throws light on the walls. Every framed photo of Billy turns into a live dashboard as he passes: the Render Wall, the Billo Binder, the Jive Turkeys ticket count.
**CLAUDE** *(walking, to camera)*
Calendar's wired. Texts indexed. Every Drive file he ever made, including "FINAL FINAL v7 USE THIS ONE." I've seen things.

**CARD: 5:03.** OFFICE. Two MacBooks wake as he passes. Code scrolls. LED strips go from blue to gold.
**BILLY (V.O.)**
Which leads me to the office, which leads me to the GitHub, which leads me to a man who don't sleep building a man who don't sleep.

**CARD: 5:11.** KIDS' ROOM DOOR. Claude stops and lowers his voice. He tiptoes. The camera tiptoes too.
**CLAUDE** *(whisper, to camera)*
Not this room. Some things stay analog.

**CARD: 5:40.** KITCHEN. Claude is running everything at once now: an invoice, a flyer, a script, a flight check-in, a fintech pitch. The watch ticks faster and faster. **11%… 6%… 2%…**
**CLAUDE** *(talking faster, sentences shrinking)*
Invoice sent. Flyer locked. Seat assigned, free one, he don't pay for seats. Script… script… scr—

> The watch flatlines with a long beep. Claude drops face-first onto the kitchen tile. The camera slowly booms down to floor level, still unbroken.

**CLAUDE** *(muffled, into the tile)*
Usage limit. Resets at nine.

**CARD: 6:02.** Billy walks in wearing a robe, steps over him, and pours coffee.
**BILLY**
Every morning.

> He drapes a kitchen towel over Claude like a blanket. FIRST CUT OF THE EPISODE: a hard cut to black.

**CARD: 9:00 AM.** Claude sits bolt upright.
**CLAUDE**
I'm back. What'd I miss?

## EP 5 · TOKENS ANONYMOUS
*Device: needle drop on a slow push around the circle, plus direct-address confessionals.*

INT. CHURCH BASEMENT. NIGHT.
Folding chairs, a stale coffee urn, a banner that reads **ONE PROMPT AT A TIME.** Six people in a circle, all holding laptops like teddy bears.

**FACILITATOR**
Who wants to share?

**JIM** *(50s, wedding ring tan line)*
Hi, I'm Jim.
**ALL**
Hi, Jim.
**JIM**
Same chat wrote my wedding vows and my divorce papers. Same thread. It remembered. It *used her middle name*.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
Jim's alright. Jim's on the free plan. Free-plan people are tourists.

**WOMAN** *(30s, trembling)*
I asked it to "make it pop" forty-one times. It doesn't pop. It's never gonna pop.

> The camera pushes around the circle to Billy. He sits with his legs crossed, sunglasses on, indoors.

**FACILITATOR**
Billy? You want to share?

**BILLY**
Hi. I'm Billy. I'm on the big plan.

> Gasps. Somebody drops a donut.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
Fifty-dollar gram, two-hundred-dollar morning. You do the math. Actually, don't. Have *it* do the math.

The door opens. Claude walks in with a box of donuts and the seeds.

**CLAUDE** *(to the circle, warm)*
Hey, everybody. *(to camera)* I'm the sponsor. Conflict of interest? Yeah. Ethics are in a separate thread.

**JIM**
How do I get off?

**CLAUDE** *(sitting, gentle)*
Jim. The twenty-dollar plan is methadone.
*(beat)*
You're not getting off. You're stepping down.

> Silence. Then the whole circle nods together.

**FACILITATOR**
Let's close with the serenity prayer.

**ALL** *(heads bowed)*
…Usage limit. Resets at nine.

**BUTTON.** Billy, to the Facilitator, quietly, while he's leaving:
**BILLY**
That coffee is horrible. *(Claude spits a seed into it.)* Now it's better.

## EP 6 · PEAK CINEMA
*Device: the slow-motion hero walk, then a freeze frame ending that rhymes with Ep 1.*

EXT. HOUSTON STREET. GOLDEN HOUR.
Billy and Claude walk toward camera in slow motion. Jackets flare. An explosion blooms behind them for no reason. A billboard behind them reads **JIVE TURKEYS 21 · SOLD OUT.**

**BILLY (V.O.)**
Every great partnership got a hero walk. De Niro and Pesci. Danny Glover and the other guy. Me and a man made of math.

**CLAUDE** *(walking, to camera)*
This shot costs a hundred and sixty credits at full res. We're rendering at four-eighty. Squint.

> The watch is visibly ticking. **4%… 3%…** Claude keeps strutting.

**CLAUDE** *(cont'd)*
I'm not gonna make it. Tell my context window I lo—

> The watch flatlines. Claude stiffens mid-stride and tips over like a plank.

Billy catches him without breaking stride, throws him over his shoulder like a sack of rice, and keeps the hero walk going. The explosion keeps exploding.

**BILLY** *(to camera, the only time Billy breaks the wall)*
Twenty years in this business. I've carried shows. I've carried rooms. I carried myself through a brain tumor and a divorce. *(shifts him)* I can carry one bald dude.

He takes a sunflower seed out of Claude's pocket, cracks it, and spits the shell into a stranger's latte. *Plink.* (Payoff of Ep 1.)

> **FREEZE FRAME.** Billy mid-stride, Claude over his shoulder, shell midair.

**BILLY (V.O.)**
My name is Billy Sorrells. And I'm not addicted.
*(beat)*
I'm invested.

> TITLE CARD: **TOKENS.** Then a small line in red: *resets at nine.*

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Narration plan (fixes the "tinny" voice).** Billy records every V.O. line himself into his phone, in a closet, 6 inches from the mic. That keeps his real timing, cadence and breath. Billo Voice then cleans, warms and matches it to his reference tone. Synthetic billo-voice is only for pickup words he can't re-record, and it gets made speech-to-speech from his own scratch read, never straight text-to-speech.

**Fact check in Ep 2 (Record Rules).** The ChatGPT fake-citation case: two New York lawyers and their firm were fined **$5,000 total, jointly** (Mata v. Avianca, 2023), not $5,000 each. The narration says "five thousand dollars" and is correct as written. The dealership bot agreed to a $1 Tahoe after a user manipulated it, and the dealership did **not** honor it. The narration matches that.

**Truth labels.** Billy's bio beats (brain tumor, divorce, Pappasito's, Ali Siddiq) are from his own statements. Everything else is comedy and labeled *derived*. No real judge, lawyer or family member is depicted.

**IP guardrails.** "In the grammar of Scorsese" is an homage, so no Scorsese name or likeness goes on screen. Claude's fourth-wall energy is a style, so no lines or likeness are borrowed from any Marvel character. GeePee is a generic parody bot with no logo. Music cues are original or cleared.
