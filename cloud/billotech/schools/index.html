Donated to schools

Students make it.
Students own it.

BilloTech puts a real production studio in every student's browser: video, color, beat editing, music and journalism tools. Nothing to install, nothing for IT to image. It runs over plain HTTPS on the Chromebooks and lab machines schools already have. We donate the license. Students keep everything they make.

What students get

A studio, free

Billo Forge

Edit video with the same effects, color and beat-cut tools the paid creators use. Live now.

The student channelIn the build

A school channel for student news, music and projects, made and run by students, in the spirit of the classroom news shows and reading programs a lot of us grew up on.

Learn by makingIn the build

Games that teach the technology while students use it: how a cut works, what a color grade does, how a beat is counted, how a stream goes live.

Billo SwarmIn the build

A class renders together. Every machine in the lab takes a slice of a long render, so a 30-machine lab finishes in a fraction of the time.

For IT and administrators

Built to get through

Plain web, no installs

Every tool is a web page on HTTPS port 443. No downloads, no plugins, no peer-to-peer ports.

Work stays with the school

Footage is processed on the student's own machine and never uploaded to BilloTech. Projects save to the device or to the school's own Google Drive.

No ads

No ads, ever, on the school version. Student work is never sold.
