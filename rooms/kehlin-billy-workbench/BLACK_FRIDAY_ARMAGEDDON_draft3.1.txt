WHAT WE TOOK FROM THE TRAILERS

JOHN WICK (the legend):
- The hero barely talks. Other people tell you how dangerous he is, and they're scared while they say it. Our version: the luxury robots tell the legend of three guys who have never paid retail.
- Chapter cards. White type on black, hard in, hard out, a low boom under each one.
- The fight is one take. No cutting to hide the stunts, the camera circles at waist height and you feel every hit. No music under the fight.
- Neon night: magenta and cyan.

BAD BOYS (the buddies):
- The 360: camera low on the ground, circling the heroes while they rise into frame, orange sun flaring behind them.
- Banter buttons: every action beat ends on two guys arguing about something small.
- Hot orange highlights, teal shadows.

TRAILER CUT (both):
- Cuts speed up toward the end, landing on the drums: kick, then snare, then every hi-hat.
- Then everything stops. One full second of black, no sound. The joke lands in the silence.

FADE IN:

SHOT 1 — COLD OPEN / THE SWARM  (5s)

EXT. CITY STREET - NIGHT

[MANN: wet asphalt, 85mm compressing the swarm, handheld at shoulder height. BAD BOYS grade: hot orange highs, teal shadows.]

Three heroes in gold-and-cream CREME GAWDS SPRINT. Behind them: a SWARM of designer robots and camera drones.

FREEZE FRAME on HERO ONE mid-stride.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
(flat, deadpan)
Record scratch. Freeze frame. Yep, that's us. You're probably wondering how three grown men ended up running from a handbag.

SHOT 2 — THE LEGEND  (6s)

INT. LUXURY BOT HQ - NIGHT

[WICK: locked wide, one cold top light, magenta and cyan neon. No score. Room tone. One ice cube settles in a glass nobody is drinking.]

GUCCHI, PRADROID and the HUNT PACK around a marble table. Nobody looks at anybody.

PRADROID
Those three. You know who they are?

GUCCHI
Nobody knows.

PRADROID
They have never paid retail. Not once. Not even at the airport.

GUCCHI
...Then who are they?

PRADROID
They're the reason we have a clearance rack.

CARD: CHAPTER ONE · RETAIL  (1.5s, low boom)

SHOT 3 — HERO ONE VS. GUCCHI  (9s, ONE TAKE)

EXT. STREET CORNER - NIGHT

[WICK: one unbroken shot. Camera orbits the fight at waist height. Real hits, no music. BRUCKHEIMER button: drop to 120 fps on the head pop.]

GUCCHI
Your outfit is... retail.

HERO ONE
(mid-fight, not slowing down)
Your outfit is a payment plan.

He turns to camera WHILE STILL FIGHTING.

HERO ONE (CONT'D)
Relax. That's not the real one. The real one has lawyers.

POP. Gucchi's head arcs in slow motion into a trash can. Swish.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
Legal made us say that. Twice.

CARD: CHAPTER TWO · TIP INCLUDED  (1.5s, low boom)

SHOT 4 — HERO TWO & THE PRADROID  (5s)

INT. PRADROID CAB → EXT. STREET

[MANN: locked dash cam, city light sliding on glass.]

PRADROID
Your fare tonight is four thousand dollars. Plus tip.

HERO TWO
For four miles?

PRADROID
The seat is leather.

HERO TWO
The seat is CRYING.

He kicks the door and leaps. FREEZE FRAME mid-air.

HERO TWO (CONT'D)
(to camera, frozen)
Do not try this. I'm a professional. I'm also uninsured.

CARD: CHAPTER THREE · PRICE UPON REQUEST  (1.5s, low boom)

EXT. STREET - NIGHT (MONTAGE)

[TRAILER RAMP: shots shrink 1 s, 0.75, 0.5, 0.33, then 4 frames each, hitting the kick, then the snare, then every hi-hat. Lands on the BAD BOYS 360: low camera circles the three as they rise into frame, orange flare behind.]
