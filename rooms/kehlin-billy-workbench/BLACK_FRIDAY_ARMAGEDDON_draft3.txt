HOW EACH ONE WOULD SHOOT IT

MICHAEL MANN (the night):
Shoot Houston downtown at 2 a.m. for real, wet the streets. Sodium orange against cyan. Long lenses compress the swarm. The camera sits at shoulder height and moves like a person, not a crane. No score in the chase, just city hum and a synth pulse. The quiet before every hit is louder than the hit.

JERRY BRUCKHEIMER (the scale):
Bigger than the budget. Low angles, a 360 orbit on the punch, 120 fps on anything that breaks. Golden backlight blasting through the showroom door like sunrise at midnight. Slow-motion hero walk at the end with something exploding behind them for no reason. Cut the chase every second and a half.

KEVIN FEIGE (the universe):
This isn't an ad, it's the origin story of the Creme Gawds Cinematic Universe. Each hero is a product line with a color: gold hoodie, cream puffer, gold tracksuit. The luxury robots are the recurring villains. Easter eggs: Whip's car parked on the corner, "Sipping" leaking out of a club door. Phase One is Black Friday. The post-credit scene sets up Character 4 for Cyber Monday.

FADE IN:

SHOT 1 — COLD OPEN / THE SWARM  (6s)

EXT. CITY STREET - NIGHT

[MANN: wet asphalt, sodium and cyan, 85mm compressing the swarm, handheld at shoulder height. City hum, no music.]

Three heroes in gold-and-cream CREME GAWDS SPRINT down the street. Behind them: a SWARM of designer robots and camera drones.

FREEZE FRAME on HERO ONE mid-stride, a sweat drop hanging in the air.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
(flat, deadpan)
Record scratch. Freeze frame. Yep, that's us. You're probably wondering how three grown men ended up running from a handbag.

UNFREEZE.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
Simple. We told the luxury brands what a hoodie should cost.

The three SPLIT. Three escapes, one destination.

SHOT 2 — HERO ONE VS. GUCCHI  (6s)

EXT. STREET CORNER - NIGHT

[BRUCKHEIMER: low angle, the robot towering against a backlit billboard, sparks, a 360 orbit, 120 fps on the head pop.]

A chrome GUCCHI ROBOT steps in and scans HERO ONE (gold hoodie) head to toe.

GUCCHI
(synthetic, snooty)
Your outfit is... retail.

HERO ONE
Your outfit is a payment plan.

One clean strike. The head POPS OFF in slow motion, spins, and drops into a trash can. Nothing but net.

HERO ONE
(to camera)
Relax. That's not the real one. The real one has lawyers.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
Legal made us say that. Twice.

SHOT 3 — HERO TWO & THE PRADROID  (6s)

INT. PRADROID CAB - NIGHT

[MANN: the cab scene, locked dash camera, city lights sliding across the glass, polite menace.]

HERO TWO (cream puffer) in the back seat. The PRADROID driver doesn't turn around.

PRADROID
(calm, courteous)
Your fare tonight is four thousand dollars. Plus tip.

HERO TWO
For four miles?

PRADROID
The seat is leather.

HERO TWO
The seat is CRYING.

He shoulder-charges the door. FREEZE FRAME mid-leap.

HERO TWO (V.O.)
(frozen, to us)
Do not try this. I'm a professional. I'm also uninsured.

UNFREEZE. EXT. STREET - CONTINUOUS. He rolls, pops up and dusts off the puffer.

HERO TWO
Not a scratch. This puffer is tougher than my credit.

SHOT 4 — HERO THREE & THE HUNT PACK  (7s)

EXT. ALLEY - NIGHT

[MANN: long-lens tracking through chain-link and steam vents. BRUCKHEIMER: a car flips behind them for no reason.]

HERO THREE (gold tracksuit) sprints. Three oversized robots THUNDER behind him, each calling out a price tag like a war cry.

Y.S. HELL
Seven-hundred-dollar BELT.

LOUIE V-9000
Twelve-hundred-dollar SLIDES.

DIE-OR
Price available... upon request.

HERO THREE
If you gotta ASK, you got ROBBED!

Behind them a parked car FLIPS in slow motion and explodes. Hero Three looks back, then at us, betrayed.

HERO THREE
(to camera)
Who approved the car? We don't have car money!

NARRATOR (V.O.)
We spent the whole budget on that car. That's why the robots are cardboard in the back. Don't look at the back.

SHOT 5 — SHOWROOM REVEAL  (5s)

INT. CREME GAWDS SHOWROOM - NIGHT

[BRUCKHEIMER: golden backlight floods the doorway, sunrise in a store at midnight, slow dolly-in through haze. FEIGE: the Creme Gawds crest over the threshold.]

All three cross. The robots SLAM to a stop at the doorway like they've hit glass.

GUCCHI'S HEAD
(muffled, from the trash can)
We can't go in there. Everything's... fairly priced.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
Luxury robots can't survive a reasonable markup. Science.

SHOT 6 — HERO WALK + TAGLINE  (6s)

INT. SHOWROOM - CONTINUOUS

[BRUCKHEIMER: slow-motion hero walk to camera, the robots outside implode in a gold flash behind them. MANN: hard cut to silence on the last line.]

THE HEROES

Creme Gawds over everything.

The HYPE MC steps in beside them: BILLY SORRELLS, as himself, deadpan.

BILLY (MC)
Including this commercial. Which I'm in. Because Kehlin owes me money.
