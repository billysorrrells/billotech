Where Whip needs to sit · what the pre-mix did
  • Lead was buried. It sat 5.3 dB under the beat and 3.9 dB under it in the 1–5 kHz range where a voice cuts through. The pre-mix brings it level with the beat and carves a 2.5 dB pocket at 3 kHz in the beat so she sits on top of it instead of behind it.
  • Lead was muddy. Too much 200–500 Hz and not enough presence. Cut 4.5 dB at 300 Hz and 1.5 dB at 800 Hz, plus 3.5 dB at 3.5 kHz and a 3 dB air shelf at 10 kHz.
  • Key correction to B minor, kept soft on the lead (85%) so it still sounds like her and tighter on the hooks (90%). Ad-libs left natural.
  • R&B stack. Hooks panned slightly left and right, the four dubs spread wide behind them, stacks centered and tucked; ad-libs and "Ugh" thrown wide with a quarter-note delay (395 ms at 152).
  • Space. Plate-style reverb send on the vocals and a quarter-note delay throw, both filtered so they don't cloud the low end.
  • Beat. Rumble cut at 28 Hz, 1.5 dB off at 250 Hz, a light filter at 17 kHz. The sample remake is tucked under at −26 LUFS.
  • Preview master is loud for listening (peaks at −1 dB). The final master gets its own pass once the mix is approved.

Every setting is saved in the session file so the same mix comes out every time. Change something with the faders here, then tell us with the Speaker Box.
